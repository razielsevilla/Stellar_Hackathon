# Stellar Philippines Hackathon 2026

> 🗓️ **May 18–24, 2026** · 🌐 **100% Online** · 👥 **Individuals & Teams Welcome**

A 7-day online hackathon bringing together developers, designers, and founders to build real-world financial solutions on the Stellar blockchain — and make money work better for every Filipino.

---

## 🧭 What Is This?

The **Stellar Philippines Hackathon 2026** is a builder-first event designed to onboard and empower the next generation of Filipino fintech innovators. Over 6 days, participants will ideate, build, and pitch MVPs that tackle real financial challenges — from remittances and financial inclusion to stablecoins and MSME tools.

Whether you're a seasoned Web3 developer or exploring blockchain for the first time, this hackathon is your launchpad.

> 💬 No prior blockchain experience required. Just bring your ideas and willingness to build.

---

## 🌍 Why Build on Stellar?

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| ⚡ Fast & Low-Cost | Transactions settle in 3–5 seconds for a fraction of a cent |
| 🌐 Cross-Border | Built for global payments across currencies |
| 🏦 Financial Inclusion | Designed for the unbanked and underserved |
| 🏷️ Asset Tokenization | Tokenize real-world assets like invoices and commodities |
| 🇵🇭 Emerging Markets | Already powering fintech solutions across Southeast Asia |

## 🎯 Theme

### *Real-World Financial Solutions for Filipinos*

Build solutions that improve how money **moves**, **grows**, and is **accessed** — for OFWs, MSMEs, unbanked communities, and everyday Filipinos.

## 🔑 Focus Areas

- 💸 **Payments & Remittances** — Cheaper, faster money transfers for OFWs and families
- 🌏 **Financial Inclusion** — Wallets and tools for the unbanked
- 🪙 **Stablecoins & PayFi** — Programmable payments and payment finance solutions
- 🏪 **MSME & Commerce Tools** — Invoicing, payroll, and cashflow for small businesses
- 🛠️ **Developer Tools on Stellar** — SDKs, infrastructure, and utilities for the ecosystem

## 🧪 What to Submit

Submit the following by **May 23:**

- [ ] **Demo Video** — 2–3 minutes showing your working product
- [ ] **Pitch Deck** — Max 10 slides
- [ ] **Working MVP** *(required)*
